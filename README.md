With 23 years of experience, including 20 years specializing in AI/ML, Daekeun has worked across startups, manufacturing, FSI, and cloud, gaining deep expertise in developing and deploying AI/ML products. Daekeun holds 6 first-author patents and have led AI/ML projects that contributed to the mass production of over 20 products. 

As an AI/ML technical specialist, Daekeun has led 200+ AI/ML workloads, delivered 100+ seminars as an ML community tech leader, and mentored 20+ ML experts. While his career was deeply rooted in computer vision, his expertise now spans all AI/ML domains including GenAI, with strong focus on AIML infrastructure and fine-tuning/serving. He holds a double major in computer science and mathematics, plus a master's in computer science specializing in machine learning.
